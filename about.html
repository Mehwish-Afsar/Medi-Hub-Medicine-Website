About MediHub

A pharmacy counter, moved online — without cutting corners.

MediHub started as a simple idea: getting genuine medicine to a Pakistani household shouldn't mean a trip across town. We built an online pharmacy that keeps every promise a good neighbourhood chemist makes — real stock, a proper cold chain, and someone on the other end of the phone — and added the convenience of ordering from home.
