Frequently Asked Questions

1. Do you deliver across Pakistan?

Yes, we deliver all across Pakistan. For physical stores, please check out our Store Locator.

2. Are all products quality products?

Yes, all our products are sourced from manufacturers or their authorized distributors. We ensure to maintain the cold chain and our inventory system ensures no fake/expired product is sold from our outlet/delivery channel.

3. How many stores?

Please check out our store locator.

4. How to order?

You can place your order by calling/WhatsApp 03-111-62-63-64, or using our website or the MediHub App available at relevant app stores.

5. How do I make an account?

You can go to Create Account and make an account there. By creating an account, you will let MediHub serve you better every time you log in. You will have a quick cart re-fill, will be able to revisit your order history, and get access to interesting discounts and promotions

6. How to order?

You can place your order by calling/WhatsApp 03-111-62-63-64, or using our website or the MediHub App available at relevant app stores.

7. How do I cancel?

You can cancel your order by calling our call center at 03-111-62-63-64. The order will be canceled in 30 minutes if it is not confirmed.

8. If my item is missing what do I do?

Please make sure you checked your order completely, and then contact us at our website.

9. Do you have a Cold chain?

Yes, all our products are stored in a dedicated warehouse with cold-chain facilities. We also deliver with necessary temperature controls so your products can reach you safely, without any problems.

10. Is online payment an option?

Yes, you can pay via credit or debit cards.

11. How long does delivery take?

Within our store locations, we deliver on the same day in 2-3 hours. For outside our store stations, delivery will take 24-48 hours.

12. Delivery charges?

Rupees 200/- delivery charges apply on orders for Karachi. For outstation, the delivery fee is charged according to the weight of the package and distance from the origin.

13. Is Exchange possible?

For Karachi, please visit the store your order came from for an exchange. For outside Karachi, an exchange will not be possible.

14. Do I need to upload a prescription?

For prescription drugs, a prescription is mandatory. Otherwise, it isn’t.

15. How is return happening?

For Karachi, please visit the store your order came from, for an exchange within 3 days of the order received. For outside Karachi, an exchange will not be possible. Original receipt is mandatory for return, exchange, and any complaint you may have. To be eligible for a return, your item must be unused and in the same condition that you received it. It must also be in the original packaging. Refrigerated items, loose tablets or capsules, syrups, inhalers, sprays, appliances, baby care and baby milk are neither returnable nor exchangeable.
